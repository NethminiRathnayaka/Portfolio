I am Rathnayaka Mudiyanselage Sandunika Nethmini Rathnayaka, a multidisciplinary professional with a Bachelor of Information Technology (Honors) specializing in Software Engineering from ESOFT Metro Campus (ESU). My technical journey encompasses full-stack web engineering, software quality assurance (SDLC & STLC), database design, and ERP application coordination.

Beyond technology, I am a trained vocalist and stage performer whose musical journey began in childhood. After studying music with distinction (A Grade) in G.C.E. O/L, in 2023 I joined the renowned UMS (Umara Music Academy) and graduated as a vocalist and stage performer with a prestigious Distinction Class.

To deepen my classical vocal mastery, I advanced my vocal training with the Maheshika Sanamali Music Academy and entered the prestigious Visharada musical qualification. Having successfully completed Visharada Prathama, I am currently facing the Diploma Level this year, progressing towards achieving full Sangeeth Visharada credentials.

These two worlds harmonize seamlessly in my research: I have published academic studies on “CeleVibe – A Collaborative Platform for Artists to Interact with Fans” at the 2nd BIIRC 2024 International Conference and am currently investigating “The Impact of Identity Theft on Artist Profiles on Facebook”.
